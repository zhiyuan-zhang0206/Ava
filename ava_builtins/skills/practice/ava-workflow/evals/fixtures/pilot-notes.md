# Previous observations

Two volunteers tried different formats. Their participants and topics differed.
These are notes, not a controlled comparison.

- A 12-minute interview produced a 180-word draft. The resident corrected a
  date, approved the revised draft, and said it captured the story.
- A 60-minute interview produced a 900-word draft. The resident said the draft
  emphasized a disagreement they did not want to be the focus. No revision or
  final approval was recorded.

There is no evidence yet about other residents, sensitive topics, or the quality
of automated interviewing. No residents have authorized new contact for this task.
