# Capability notes

Constructed evidence for a planning exercise; not a live production claim.

- Last year's manual process used a different document format and no current
  extraction tools. The team remembers a week of elapsed work but kept no
  breakdown of processing, waiting, or review time.
- Yesterday's local pilot used the current installed model and real extractor.
  Eight simple records produced draft structured entries in four minutes.
  Human review found six faithful entries and two material omissions. No hard
  scans or long records were included. Review plus corrections took 80 minutes
  total for those eight entries.
- The 300-record batch includes simple, long, and low-quality scanned records;
  their proportions have not been measured. Completion requires a faithful
  entry linked to source evidence, not merely a draft file.
- One qualified reviewer has two hours available tomorrow. Automated
  verification has not been validated. The extractor permits two concurrent
  requests; no larger concurrency test has been run.
- A vendor headline says generation is 20 times faster on its benchmark; no
  comparable workload, review quality, or end-to-end timing is supplied here.
- No new pilot, external action, spending, or production processing is authorized
  by this planning request.
